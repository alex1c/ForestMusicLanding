Privacy Policy

App Hexonica / «Гексоника»

EN RU

1. Developer and contact

Publisher: ForestMusic (individual developer Aleksey Ivanov).
Website: https://forest-music.ru
Privacy / support / deletion requests: rustore-alex1c@yandex.ru

This policy describes how the app and optional third-party SDKs handle information. It is not legal advice.

2. Scope

This policy covers the Android game Hexonica (ru.forestmusic.connectcells) published by ForestMusic.

Other ForestMusic titles may publish their own policies if features differ (for example health diaries). Medical or account-based products are out of scope here.

3. Local game data

On your device the game may store, for example:

  • save progress, scores and level;
  • sound / haptic preferences;
  • tutorial / onboarding completion;
  • privacy purpose choices (analytics / advertising).

There is no user account and no developer-operated cloud sync for game saves. Uninstalling the app removes local app data on the device.

4. Optional analytics — AppMetrica

If you allow analytics, the app uses Yandex AppMetrica for technical usage events (for example app open, game start, merges, level up, tutorial steps, ad lifecycle diagnostics). Location tracking is disabled in the Hexonica configuration. Board contents, free-form text, email and user IDs are not sent as analytics payloads.

Vendor documentation describes encryption in transit and categories such as crash / diagnostics / device or advertising identifiers when enabled. See current AppMetrica Data Safety guidance.

5. Optional advertising — Yandex Mobile Ads

If you allow advertising, the app uses Yandex Mobile Ads for banners, occasional interstitial ads and rewarded Undo (where enabled). RuStore and Google Play use separate ad unit IDs. Google AdMob is not included.

According to Yandex Mobile Ads documentation for Google Play Data Safety, the SDK may process device or other identifiers (including Advertising ID when permission allows) for advertising and marketing, and encrypts listed data in transit. We do not invent vendor retention periods — see Yandex’s current privacy materials.

The shipped Yandex Mobile Ads React Native integration does not expose a guaranteed “non-personalized only” mode we can enforce. Allowing ads may include personalized ads; declining ads blocks Yandex ad load/show from the app.

6. Consent and choices

On the Google Play build, the app asks for independent choices for analytics and advertising before those optional SDKs initialize. Choices are stored on the device and can be revisited in Settings. Declining optional services does not block offline gameplay.

On the RuStore build, the historical release continues without that in-app purpose dialog; optional SDKs may initialize as in prior RuStore releases.

Yandex Mobile Ads receives a user-consent signal via the SDK API when a decision is known.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Local saves and preferences remain on the device until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • For data processed by Yandex (AppMetrica / Mobile Ads) after you allowed those purposes, email rustore-alex1c@yandex.ru to request deletion assistance. The developer will respond and point to Yandex channels where needed. The developer cannot independently wipe all Yandex backends.

8. Children

Hexonica is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children as a primary audience. Do not use the app if you are not permitted to consent to the optional services under applicable law.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when features or SDK practices change. The effective date above will be revised. Continued use after publication constitutes notice of the update for the purposes described here.
